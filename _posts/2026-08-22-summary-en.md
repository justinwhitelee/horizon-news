### [Open-source roguelike DelveRL released for reinforcement-learning agent training](https://www.reddit.com/r/MachineLearning/comments/1vvii1j/i_built_an_opensource_roguelike_specifically_for/) ⭐️ 8.0/10



reddit · r/MachineLearning · /u/SnyderConsulting · Aug 22, 17:32

#### Summary

A developer released DelveRL, an open-source deterministic roguelike built specifically to lower the integration barrier for training and benchmarking reinforcement-learning agents. The game provides a structured API, procedural levels, partial observability, and a local recurrent PPO trainer, addressing the difficulty of adapting most commercial or complex games to agent harnesses. A included baseline reaches a median floor of 18, with extended runs progressing to floor 33, and all training code, checkpoints, and raw benchmarks are publicly available for community evaluation.

### [Open-source models closing gap faster each generation, SemiAnalysis finds](https://newsletter.semianalysis.com/p/are-open-models-catching-up) ⭐️ 8.0/10



telegram · zaihuapd · Aug 22, 08:26

#### Summary

SemiAnalysis analyzes the accelerating gap between open-source and closed-source AI models across three historical eras — early scaling, reasoning, and agentic — finding that open models close the performance gap in half the time with each generation. In the current agentic era, Kimi K2.6 surpassed Claude Opus 4.5 in just 4.8 months, and GLM-5.2 overtook GPT-5.2 in 6 months. Models like GLM 5.3 and Kimi K3 can now handle many programming and agent tasks that previously gave Anthropic a competitive edge worth over $650 billion in annualized revenue. However, SemiAnalysis cautions that benchmarks are not everything, and Anthropic retains a productization advantage.
