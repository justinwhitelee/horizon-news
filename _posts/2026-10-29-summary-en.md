### [Apple 新款 MacBook Pro 首发 PROLED XDR 显示屏并加入触控](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/10/apple-unveils-groundbreaking-macbook-pro-with-proled-xdr-display/) ⭐️ 8.0/10



telegram · zaihuapd · Oct 28, 17:16

#### 摘要

Apple 发布新款 MacBook Pro，首次引入 PROLED XDR 串联 OLED 显示屏，峰值亮度达 2000 尼特，对比度为 1000 万:1，这是 Mac 产品线首次支持触控输入。新机搭载 M5 Pro 和 M5 Max 芯片，提供 14 英寸和 16 英寸两种规格，机身更薄更轻，电池续航最长可达 24 小时。10 月 28 日起开启预售，11 月 6 日正式开售，14 英寸版本起售价为 22999 人民币，16 英寸版本起售价为 27999 人民币。

#### Background

PROLED XDR \(Extreme Dynamic Range\) refers to Apple&\#x27;s tandem OLED display technology, which stacks two OLED panels to achieve significantly higher brightness and contrast compared to traditional single-panel OLEDs. XDR displays have become a premium feature on professional monitors and laptops, delivering deep blacks alongside high peak brightness for creative work. Apple has historically kept touch input exclusive to iPadOS devices; this MacBook Pro marks the first time touchscreen interaction has been introduced to the macOS lineup. The M5 Pro and M5 Max chips are part of Apple&\#x27;s fifth-generation custom Silicon family, succeeding the M4 series with enhanced CPU and GPU performance for professional workflows.
