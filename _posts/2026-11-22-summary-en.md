#### Background

The Riemann Hypothesis, proposed by Bernhard Riemann in 1859, concerns the distribution of prime numbers and remains one of mathematics&\#x27; most famous unsolved problems. Formal verification uses machine-checked proof assistants like Lean to verify mathematical proofs with absolute certainty, eliminating human error. Lean&\#x27;s Mathlib project has been building a comprehensive library of formalized mathematics over the past decade, making large-scale proofs increasingly feasible.
