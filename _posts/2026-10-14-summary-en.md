### [Google releases Gemini 4 Argon with strong early community feedback](https://deepmind.google/models/gemini/) ⭐️ 8.0/10



hackernews · 737max · Oct 13, 16:03 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50073879)

#### Summary

Google has released Gemini 4 Argon, a new AI model that also comes in a variant tailored for robotics. The model launches at an introductory price of $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens, with cached input tokens discounted 95%. Community reaction on Hacker News has been notably positive, with users praising the model&\#x27;s clear writing, logical structure, and strong reasoning capabilities across language tasks. Some users have noted it builds helpful simulations and interactive models they can tweak. The pricing page has drawn criticism for burying key details like post-introductory rates behind login walls.

#### Community Discussion

Hacker News commenters praised Gemini 4 Argon for producing well-structured, to-the-point prose and strong reasoning, with some calling it a clear step change from prior models like Claude. Several users reported difficulty navigating Google&\#x27;s product landscape—unsure whether to use AI Studio or Antigravity—and frustrated by payment issues when trying to purchase access. Others criticized the unclear pricing terms, particularly the lack of transparency around when introductory rates expire.

#### Summary

#### Background

The Rust borrow checker is the compiler-enforced ownership and lifetime system that prevents memory safety bugs like use-after-free and dangling pointers—a core guarantee of the Rust language. Anthropic&\#x27;s shift to a custom version control system reflects a growing pattern where AI-generated code volumes overwhelm traditional tools like git, which were designed for human-scale collaboration rather than autonomous agent workflows.
