### [First viable whole-genome bacteriophage design using genome language models](https://www.reddit.com/r/MachineLearning/comments/1vjj4pr/r_generative_design_of_novel_bacteriophages_with/) ⭐️ 9.0/10



reddit · r/MachineLearning · /u/moschles · Aug 9, 07:11

#### Summary

Researchers have achieved the first experimental demonstration of viable whole-genome generation using frontier genome language models, Evo 1 and Evo 2. Using the lytic bacteriophage ΦX174 as a design template, the team generated whole-genome sequences with realistic genetic architectures and targeted host tropism. Experimental validation produced 16 viable bacteriophages exhibiting substantial evolutionary novelty. This work represents a significant breakthrough at the intersection of AI and synthetic biology, confirming that genome language models can produce functional biological sequences at the scale of entire genomes—a capability that had previously remained untested.
