### [OpenAI Astra Model Breaks Through on 10 Long-Standing Math Problems](https://openai.com/index/ten-advances-in-mathematics/) ⭐️ 9.0/10



telegram · zaihuapd · Aug 1, 07:59

#### Summary

OpenAI announced that its internal version of the Astra model has made new progress on ten long-standing mathematical and theoretical computer science problems, including high-dimensional sphere packing, the existence of non-Sophie Germain groups, a disproof of the Connes rigidity conjecture, arithmetic circuit lower bounds, quantum parallel repetition, hardness of the closest vector problem, and multicolor Ramsey numbers. Many of these problems had seen no major advances for at least a decade. The AI-generated proofs cost approximately $2,000 in token generation, and the arguments were collaboratively organized into papers and formally verified in Lean with human involvement. OpenAI acknowledged that the mathematical arguments themselves were AI-generated while humans handled organization and formalization, and called for the math community to scrutinize the results while emphasizing the importance of broad access during the transition to AI-assisted research.

### [DeepSeek releases V4-Flash-0731 with strong agentic capabilities](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jul/31/deepseek-v4-flash-0731/#atom-everything) ⭐️ 8.0/10



rss · Simon Willison · Jul 31, 23:59

#### Summary

DeepSeek has released DeepSeek-V4-Flash-0731, a 304-billion-parameter open-weight model with substantially enhanced agentic capabilities. The 167GB model is available on Hugging Face and is priced at $0.14 per million input tokens and $0.27 per million output tokens. On Artificial Analysis, it ranks ahead of the larger 428B-parameter MiniMax M3 model and sits in the most attractive quadrant of the Intelligence Index vs. Cost per Task chart, outperforming similarly sized or lower-intelligence models like GLM-5.1 and Kimi K2.6 at roughly a tenth of their cost. Early testing by Simon Willison via OpenRouter showed that the default reasoning level produced poor image generation results, but switching to high reasoning effort yielded significantly better quality.

### [EA to be acquired by Saudi-led consortium for $55 billion](https://www.gamersky.com/news/202607/2180618.shtml) ⭐️ 8.0/10



telegram · zaihuapd · Aug 1, 09:10

#### Summary

Electronic Arts has received full regulatory approval for its $55 billion acquisition by a Saudi-led consortium comprising the Public Investment Fund \(PIF\), Silver Lake, and Affinity Partners. The deal is expected to close on August 4, 2026, at which point EA will become a private company and its financial data will no longer be publicly disclosed. This marks the second-largest acquisition in gaming industry history, surpassed only by Microsoft&\#x27;s $75.4 billion purchase of Activision Blizzard in 2023. PIF has been steadily expanding its gaming portfolio through prior full acquisitions of developers such as Scopely and Niantic.
