### [China&\#x27;s 800MW Wind-Powered AI Data Center Campus in Inner Mongolia](https://newsletter.semianalysis.com/p/we-visited-chinas-stargate-in-inner) ⭐️ 8.0/10



rss · Semianalysis · Nov 7, 19:01

#### Summary

SemiAnalysis published a site visit report on an 800-megawatt wind-powered AI data center campus in Inner Mongolia, China. The facility deployed its first 120 MW within a single year, demonstrating rapid infrastructure scaling. A 5 MW pilot for a 800V DC distribution system—native to solid-state transformers—is underway, representing a departure from conventional AC power architectures. The campus also features a chiller-free cooling design that eliminates centralized mechanical refrigeration units.

#### Background

Large-scale AI training workloads are driving unprecedented demand for data center power, often running into hundreds of megawatts per campus. Inner Mongolia has emerged as a location for energy-intensive compute due to abundant renewable wind capacity and cool ambient temperatures that reduce cooling costs. Solid-state transformer \(SST\) technology enables direct high-voltage DC distribution, potentially improving efficiency at scale compared to traditional AC-transformer-rectifier chains.
