### [SemiAnalysis Tests CUDA Moat Against AMD MI355 in Agentic Inference](https://newsletter.semianalysis.com/p/agentx-inferencexv3-does-cuda-moat) ⭐️ 8.0/10



rss · Semianalysis · Aug 24, 00:19

#### Summary

SemiAnalysis has open-sourced a $3 million dataset benchmarking agentic inference workloads to evaluate whether NVIDIA&\#x27;s CUDA ecosystem still dominates against AMD&\#x27;s MI355. The InferenceXv3 benchmark features 1M+ token context lengths, multiturn interactions, and sub-agent orchestration, achieving 95%+ KVCache hit rates. Testing runs on both NVIDIA GB300 NVL72 and B200 platforms alongside AMD MI355 hardware. The results directly assess the durability of CUDA&\#x27;s competitive moat in the growing agentic AI inference segment.
