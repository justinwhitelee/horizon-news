### [Europe launches its first commercial orbital rocket](https://www.theverge.com/science/990906/isar-aerospace-europe-orbital-rocket-launch) ⭐️ 9.0/10



rss · The Verge · Sep 6, 19:04

#### Summary

German company Isar Aerospace has successfully launched Europe&\#x27;s first entirely commercial orbital rocket, the two-stage Spectrum, into low Earth orbit from a Norwegian spaceport. The achievement marks a major milestone for European commercial spaceflight and comes after a failed attempt the previous March, when the vehicle crashed into the sea and exploded just 30 seconds into flight. The successful orbit insertion demonstrates that a purely private European launcher can reliably reach space, potentially shifting the competitive landscape for European access to orbit.

#### Background

Europe has historically relied on the European Space Agency&\#x27;s Ariane rockets for orbital access, which are government-backed rather than commercially operated. Isar Aerospace, a German startup, is attempting to establish a privately funded alternative. The Spectrum rocket is a two-stage launch vehicle designed for small-to-medium payload delivery to low Earth orbit.

### [OpenAI tracks coding agent uptake among its researchers](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Sep/6/research-acceleration-the-view-inside-openai/) ⭐️ 8.0/10



rss · Simon Willison · Sep 6, 23:57

#### Summary

OpenAI published an internal essay detailing how its research team is using coding agents to accelerate productivity, alongside another essay titled &quot;An Alien Mind&quot; by Chief Scientist Jakub Pachocki, both referencing the company&\#x27;s Recursive Self-Improvement \(RSI\) initiative. A chart in the piece shows median daily AI spend per researcher rising from near zero in February 2026 to roughly $50 by April, ~$150 by June, plateauing through July, then climbing sharply to approximately $600 by late August 2026. Blog author Simon Willison speculates the late-July inflection coincides with internal employees gaining access to the model later released as GPT-6 Astra. The post frames 2026 as the year agentic engineering took off at OpenAI.

#### Background

Recursive Self-Improvement \(RSI\) refers to AI systems that can iteratively enhance their own architectures or training processes, widely discussed as a potential path toward advanced AI. Coding agents are autonomous software-tool assistants that write, test, and iterate on code, a capability that has seen rapid adoption across the industry in 2026.
