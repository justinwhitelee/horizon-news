### [ULA&\#x27;s Vulcan Rocket Fails, Destroying 40 Amazon Kuiper Satellites](https://www.theverge.com/science/1034006/vulcan-rocket-fails-destroying-40-amazon-leo-satellites) ⭐️ 8.0/10



rss · The Verge · Nov 15, 21:05

#### Summary

United Launch Alliance \(ULA\) suffered its first mission failure in its 20-year history when its Vulcan rocket failed to deliver 40 Amazon Leo satellites to orbit. The loss represents a significant setback for Amazon Kuiper, which is building a low-earth orbit satellite constellation intended to compete with SpaceX&\#x27;s Starlink. Amazon&\#x27;s Kuiper program has already struggled to deploy sufficient satellites to mount a credible challenge to Starlink, making each payload loss more painful. This incident marks the first time ULA has lost a rocket since its founding two decades ago.

#### Background

Amazon Kuiper is a planned low-earth orbit satellite internet constellation designed to provide global broadband access and directly compete with SpaceX&\#x27;s Starlink network, which already serves thousands of customers worldwide. United Launch Alliance is a joint venture between Boeing and Lockheed Martin that has operated commercial and government launch services for 20 years without a prior mission failure.
