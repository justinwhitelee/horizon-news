### [DeepMind Deploys SL2T Sign-Language Model in Pixel 11](https://deepmind.google/blog/putting-sign-language-ai-into-users-hands/) ⭐️ 9.0/10



telegram · zaihuapd · Aug 13, 08:55

#### Summary

Google DeepMind has released SL2T, a large-scale multilingual sign-language-to-text model, marking the first time such an AI system has been integrated into consumer products. The model is now live in Pixel 11&\#x27;s Gboard keyboard and Live Transcribe app, initially supporting American Sign Language to English conversion, with additional languages and devices planned. Trained on over 100,000 hours of data spanning more than 50 sign languages, SL2T achieved a zero-shot score of 70 BLEURT on the FLEURS-ASL benchmark, significantly surpassing prior records. To protect user privacy, the model processes only hand and body pose keypoints rather than raw video input.
