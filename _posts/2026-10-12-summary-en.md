### [Zelenskyy welcomes US-brokered energy ceasefire with Russia](https://www.aljazeera.com/video/newsfeed/2026/10/11/zelenskyy-welcomes-us-announced-energy-ceasefire?traffic_source=rss) ⭐️ 8.0/10



rss · Al Jazeera · Oct 11, 20:28

#### Summary

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy has responded positively to Donald Trump&\#x27;s announcement that Russia and Ukraine agreed to an energy ceasefire. The agreement, brokered by the United States, aims to protect critical energy infrastructure during the ongoing conflict between the two nations. The development marks a significant diplomatic shift, as energy infrastructure has been a frequent target throughout the war. The ceasefire represents a US-brokered effort to de-escalate attacks on energy facilities in Eastern Europe.

#### Background

The Russia-Ukraine war has targeted energy infrastructure on both sides, with Russian forces repeatedly striking power facilities in Kyiv, Kharkiv, and other Ukrainian cities. President Volodymyr Zelenskyy had previously indicated that multiple ceasefire categories—energy, grain, and maritime—were under discussion between Kyiv and Moscow. US President Donald Trump announced the energy ceasefire agreement on his Truth Social platform, a move that reportedly surprised Zelenskyy, who stated the announcement was new to him, while Moscow had not yet commented on the deal.
