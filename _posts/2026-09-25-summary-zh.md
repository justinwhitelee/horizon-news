### [New cryptanalytic approach breaks RSA without factoring](https://arstechnica.com/security/2026/09/theres-a-new-way-to-break-rsa-thats-faster-than-anything-weve-seen-before/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Researchers have identified a novel method to compromise RSA encryption that does not rely on integer factorization, potentially making attacks faster than previously known techniques.

rss · Ars Technica · 9月24日 11:15

#### Summary

Cryptographers have developed a new technique to break RSA that bypasses traditional integer factorization, making it faster than prior methods. RSA&\#x27;s security has historically depended on the computational hardness of factoring large composite numbers, so an alternative attack path is technically significant. This advancement could shift practical threat models for widely deployed encryption systems, though details remain limited. The finding challenges the assumption that factoring was the only viable route to compromising RSA.

#### Background

RSA is an asymmetric cryptographic algorithm widely used for secure data transmission and digital signatures. Its security traditionally rests on the difficulty of factoring the product of two large prime numbers. While quantum algorithms like Shor&\#x27;s pose a future theoretical threat, classical attacks have largely focused on improving factorization efficiency.

#### 摘要

据路透社报道，Akamai 已与 Anthropic 签署一项价值 116 亿美元的云计算协议。作为交易的一部分，Akamai 获得了 Anthropic 最多 5%股权的认股权证。这笔交易是 AI 与云计算基础设施领域的重要商业合作，表明 Akamai 对 Anthropic 的 AI 能力进行了重大投资承诺，也反映了云计算基础设施提供商在扩展 AI 容量方面与领先 AI 实验室的战略合作趋势。

#### 背景

Akamai Technologies（NASDAQ: AKAM）是一家全球领先的 CDN 和云计算基础设施提供商，总部位于美国马萨诸塞州。Anthropic 是一家总部位于旧金山的人工智能研究实验室，以开发 Claude 大型语言模型而闻名。两家公司在 AI 基础设施领域存在战略交集：Anthropic 作为 AI 模型训练和推理需求方，需要大规模云算力支持；Akamai 则寻求通过绑定头部 AI 实验室来扩大其云服务业务。此类「基础设施+AI 实验室」的合作模式在行业中日益普遍，例如亚马逊 AWS 与 OpenAI、微软 Azure 与 OpenAI 均有类似的大额合作协议。
