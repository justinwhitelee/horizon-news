### [Levent Alpöge claims proof of the Generalized Riemann Hypothesis using Lean formalization](https://alpo.ge/remark.pdf) ⭐️ 9.0/10



hackernews · iamronaldo · Nov 20, 14:16 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50574968)

#### Summary

Levent Alpöge has announced a claimed proof of the Generalized Riemann Hypothesis, developed in collaboration with Claude \(Anthropic&\#x27;s AI\). The claim is backed by a massive machine-checked formalization totaling approximately 15 million lines of code across a 1.3 GB download distributed as 14 compressed parts via lean.tar.gz.part01 through part14. The formalization was built in the Lean theorem prover. A separate human-readable version of the proof reportedly exists as well. If verified correct, it would resolve one of mathematics&\#x27; most famous open problems and could carry major implications for number theory, computer science, and AI-assisted theorem proving.

#### Background

The Generalized Riemann Hypothesis \(GRH\) extends the 1859 Riemann hypothesis to all Dirichlet L‑functions; its verification would have profound consequences for prime‑number theory and many other areas of mathematics. On November 20, 2026, Levent Alpöge claimed that, working with Anthropic’s Claude model, he had derived a proof of GRH formalized entirely in the Lean theorem prover without unverified assumptions—a scale of machine‑checked formalization previously unseen \(tool-1-1, tool-2-1\). Lean is a dependently‑typed proof assistant used by the mathematics community to verify large formal arguments with computer‑assisted rigor.

#### Community Discussion

The Hacker News community has raised both excitement and skepticism. Some commenters noted the unusual speed of the announcement and speculated about whether AI companies have been secretly advancing on the problem. Others referenced Terence Tao&\#x27;s prior statement that current AI cannot solve RH and that a combination of humans and AIs may be needed decades from now, making this claim seem premature to some. Practical verification concerns were also voiced given the sheer scale of the 15M-line formalization.

### [AI and Human Independently Claim Solution to Riemann Hypothesis](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/20/ai-claimed-a-solution-to-the-167-year-old-riemann-hypothesis-and-a-human-did-too/) ⭐️ 8.0/10



rss · TechCrunch · Nov 20, 19:32

#### Summary

An AI system has independently claimed a solution to the Riemann hypothesis alongside a human mathematician, according to a November 20, 2026 TechCrunch report. The dual claims have generated significant attention in both mathematical and AI research communities as they represent competing approaches to resolving the 167-year-old open problem. Both results remain claims awaiting formal verification rather than established proofs, but their emergence highlights an evolving role for artificial intelligence in advanced mathematical research.

#### Background

The Riemann hypothesis, proposed by Bernhard Riemann in 1859, conjectures that the seemingly random distribution of prime numbers follows a hidden, intricate order. It is one of the seven Millennium Prize Problems selected by the Clay Mathematics Institute in 2000, each carrying a $1 million reward for a correct solution. A verified proof would profoundly impact number theory and our understanding of the structure underlying prime numbers.
