### [Google brings agentic AI to Gemini for businesses](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/08/google-brings-agentic-ai-to-gemini-starting-with-businesses/) ⭐️ 8.0/10



rss · TechCrunch · Oct 8, 18:18

#### Summary

Google announced agentic AI capabilities for its Gemini platform, rolling out first for business customers. The new agent can autonomously plan and execute tasks across business applications and systems, delegate work to subagents, and orchestrate multiple AI models within a single workflow. A notable addition is that each agent receives a dedicated workplace identity complete with an email address, positioning it as a stand-alone participant in enterprise communication. The move signals Google&\#x27;s push into agentic AI for the enterprise, where autonomous task delegation and multi-model coordination are increasingly expected in business workflows.
