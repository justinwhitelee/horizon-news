#### Summary

On the first anniversary of GPT-5&\#x27;s release \(August 7, 2025\), OpenAI introduced Agent Plugins, an open and vendor-neutral standard that packages Agent Skills and MCP servers into a portable plugin format for unified discovery and loading across clients. The project is openly licensed for development and governed by a steering committee that includes Amazon, Cursor, Microsoft, OpenAI, and Vercel. GPT-5 has since iterated through versions 5.1 to 5.6, with Apple integrating it into iOS 26&\#x27;s Apple Intelligence and the Codex app becoming the new ChatGPT desktop client in July 2026.
