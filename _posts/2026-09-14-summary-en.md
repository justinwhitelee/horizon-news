### [Leaked Code Suggests iOS 27 May Allow Third-Party AI Models in Siri](https://x.com/itspdfu/status/2099122424209916015) ⭐️ 8.0/10



telegram · zaihuapd · Sep 13, 13:48

#### Summary

According to an unverified Telegram post by leaker @itspdfu, reportedly leaked code in iOS 27 and macOS Golden Gate reveals a private Model Delegation API within App Intents that would let developers integrate third-party AI models into Siri. If functional, the API—guarded by the private entitlement com.apple.developer.model-delegation—would allow models such as Claude to appear in Siri&\#x27;s &quot;Ask about...&quot; menu and handle tasks like CSV generation, while delegating system-level operations such as setting reminders back to native Siri. The claim remains entirely unconfirmed, with no corroborating evidence beyond the single Telegram source.

#### Background

Apple has historically kept Siri tightly coupled to its own AI infrastructure, though the company has gradually opened App Intents as a framework for developers to expose actions to Siri in recent iOS versions. TheModel Delegation API, if real, would represent a significant departure from this approach by permitting external AI providers to serve as Siri&\#x27;s reasoning backend rather than restricting Siri to Apple-designed services.
