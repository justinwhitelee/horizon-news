#### Summary

German company Isar Aerospace has successfully launched Europe&\#x27;s first entirely commercial orbital rocket, the two-stage Spectrum, into low Earth orbit from a Norwegian spaceport. This marks a major milestone for European commercial spaceflight, coming after a failed attempt last March when the vehicle crashed into the sea and exploded just 30 seconds after liftoff. The successful mission demonstrates the growing viability of commercial launch providers in Europe&\#x27;s emerging space industry.

### [OpenAI 内部研究：编程智能体如何加速研究效率](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Sep/6/research-acceleration-the-view-inside-openai/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

#### 摘要

OpenAI 于 2026 年 9 月 6 日发布内部研究报告，展示了编码智能体（coding agents）如何重塑研究人员日常工作。报告显示，OpenAI 研究人员的日均 AI 支出从 2 月的接近零美元快速攀升，6 月达到约 150 美元，并在 8 月底急剧跃升至约 600 美元。Simon Willison 推测 7 月下旬的加速可能与内部员工获得后来发布为 GPT-6 Astra 的模型权限有关。这一趋势与 OpenAI 的递归自我改进（Recursive Self-Improvement，简称 RSI）计划密切相关，RSI 被视作 OpenAI 新一代 AGI 的核心方向。
