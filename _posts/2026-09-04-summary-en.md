### [US Government Supports OpenAI&\#x27;s Fair Use Defense in NYT Copyright Case](https://www.reuters.com/legal/litigation/us-government-backs-openai-new-york-times-copyright-case-2026-09-02/) ⭐️ 9.0/10



telegram · zaihuapd · Sep 3, 05:45

#### Summary

The US government filed its first amicus brief with a Manhattan federal court supporting OpenAI&\#x27;s fair use defense in a copyright lawsuit brought by The New York Times over the use of copyrighted articles to train ChatGPT. The brief asserts that training large language models on copyrighted content generally constitutes fair use. Although the opinion carries no binding legal authority, analysts note it could bolster the confidence of tech companies facing similar litigation. The NYT sued OpenAI and Microsoft in 2023, alleging millions of articles were used without permission. The newspaper criticized the government for siding with &quot;a handful of trillion-dollar AI companies&quot; at the expense of creators.

#### Background

Fair use is a US copyright doctrine that permits limited use of copyrighted material without permission for purposes such as criticism, commentary, or education. The four-factor test examines purpose and character of use, nature of the copyrighted work, amount used, and effect on the market. AI training data disputes have become a central legal battleground as LLM developers rely on massive corpora of web-scraped and licensed content.
