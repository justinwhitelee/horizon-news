### [OpenAI Disbands Robotics Team, Pivots to General Model-Driven Control](https://fortune.com/2026/11/13/openai-disbands-robotics-team-as-its-general-models-start-running-robots/) ⭐️ 8.0/10



telegram · zaihuapd · Nov 14, 04:00

#### Summary

OpenAI has disbanded its independent robotics team, reassigning researchers to broader general-model research groups. The company says robotics remains central to its long-term vision, but asserts that frontier models like GPT-6 Astra can now directly control robots without dedicated teams. This is not OpenAI&\#x27;s first robotics pivot: an earlier team was dissolved in 2020, rebuilt in 2024 alongside a collaboration with Figure, and that partnership ended in February 2025. OpenAI continues self-developing a humanoid robot, with CEO ambitions to unveil it publicly by 2027.
