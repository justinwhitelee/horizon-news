### [Untethered iPhone 18 Pro jailbreak demonstrated, first since iOS 9](https://x.com/calif_io/status/2111080240978223545?s=20) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Calif researcher shows persistent jailbreak without computer after reboot, exploiting cryptex, TSS, and PAC layers.

telegram · zaihuapd · Oct 17, 10:34

#### Summary

A researcher known as Calif has demonstrated an untethered jailbreak for the iPhone 18 Pro that preserves its jailbroken state across reboots without requiring a computer. This marks the first public complete jailbreak for iPhones since iOS 9 was released in 2015. The attack chain exploits vulnerabilities across three layers: the cryptex subsystem, TSS \(Ticket Signing Server\) ticket spoofing, and PAC \(Pointer Authentication Code\) protection. Apple had patched 273 security vulnerabilities just days earlier on September 14, yet Calif&\#x27;s proof-of-concept reveals additional undisclosed attack vectors. The demonstration highlights ongoing vulnerabilities in Apple&\#x27;s mobile operating system despite aggressive patching efforts.

#### Background

iOS jailbreaking refers to removing Apple&\#x27;s software restrictions on iPhone and iPad devices, granting users root-level access and the ability to install unofficial apps and tweaks. An untethered jailbreak persists across device reboots without requiring a computer connection, unlike tethered jailbreaks that must be re-applied after each restart. The last publicly available complete untethered iOS jailbreak was for iOS 9 \(released in 2015\), making any new untethered exploit for modern iOS versions a significant milestone. Apple has progressively hardened its security layers over the years, including PAC \(Pointer Authentication Codes\), the Trusted Security System \(TSS\) for ticket signing, and the cryptex filesystem subsystem, which are now targeted by this reported attack chain.
