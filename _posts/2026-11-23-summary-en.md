### [OpenAI finds perplexity still predicts intelligence despite RL-era training](https://openai.com/index/perplexity-and-intelligence/) ⭐️ 8.0/10



hackernews · acossta · Nov 22, 17:06 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50601525)

#### Summary

OpenAI published an analysis demonstrating that perplexity—a measure of how surprised a model is when predicting the next token—remains a strong predictor of overall model intelligence even as reinforcement learning \(RL\)-based training methods have become more prominent. The finding was described by readers as surprising, since many had assumed cross-entropy loss would lose relevance once RL took center stage. The authors noted that perplexity offers a simple, consistent benchmark compared with increasingly convoluted evaluation suites. Microsoft has also joined the effort, reporting its MAI-2 Base model achieves a log-perplexity of 0.819 on OpenAI&\#x27;s held-out evaluation set with OpenAI&\#x27;s permission.

#### Community Discussion

Commenters highlighted that perplexity intuitively captures a model&\#x27;s ability to build world models from raw data, noting a theoretical argument dating back to a 2008 paper. Several expressed shock that the correlation persists so cleanly despite the rise of reasoning-focused RL models like o1. One reader asked about GPT-4.5 and whether o1 marked the beginning of true reasoning models. Microsoft&\#x27;s public adoption of the same evaluation set was seen as a sign of growing industry consensus around standardized perplexity tracking.

### [Ant Group leads $100M pre-A in Simplexity Robotics at $1.6B valuation](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-22/ant-group-bets-on-simplexity-to-thrive-in-china-s-humanoid-race) ⭐️ 8.0/10



telegram · zaihuapd · Nov 23, 02:02

#### Summary

Simplexity Robotics has completed a $100 million pre-A funding round led by Ant Group, valuing the company at $1.6 billion. Existing investors Alibaba, Tencent, HongShan, and BlueRun Ventures participated in the follow-on investment, while chairman and co-founder Wang Kai confirmed the deal to Bloomberg. The company was founded in July 2025 in Hangzhou, with its core team recruited from Li Auto; Jia Peng serves as CEO and Wang Jiajia as COO. In March 2025, Simplexity disclosed it had already raised 2 billion yuan across five rounds within six months at a post-money valuation exceeding $1 billion. Bloomberg noted that Chinese humanoid robots account for approximately 97% of global shipments, and Simplexity aims to compete with more mature rivals such as Unitree and UBTECH.

#### Background

Embodied AI refers to intelligent systems that operate through physical bodies, with humanoid robots being a leading application combining large language models with advanced motor control. China&\#x27;s humanoid robotics sector has seen explosive growth in 2024–2025, driven by state support, deep tech talent, and massive domestic manufacturing scale, which Bloomberg says accounts for roughly 97% of global shipments. Established players include Hangzhou-based Unitree Robotics and Shenzhen-based UBTECH Robotics, both of which have shipped thousands of units.
