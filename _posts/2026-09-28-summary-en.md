### [China&\#x27;s 24GW Data-Center Capacity Surpasses EMEA and Rest-of-APAC Combined](https://newsletter.semianalysis.com/p/the-chinese-ai-infrastructure-boom) ⭐️ 8.0/10

#### Summary

SemiAnalysis&\#x27;s latest model estimates that China&\#x27;s delivered data-center capacity has exceeded 24GW, encompassing over 60 operators and 1,000+ facilities — now surpassing the combined total of EMEA and the rest of APAC. The growth is driven largely by rapid upgrades of previously underutilized retail机房 through high-density electrical infrastructure and liquid-cooling retrofits, converting them into dense AI clusters. ByteDance alone accounts for roughly 20% of delivered capacity and achieved a record 100MW deployment within 12 months at core nodes. Meanwhile, Alibaba, Tencent, and Baidu combined their Q2 2026 capex to an estimated $20 billion — doubling year-over-year — and for the first time all three recorded negative free cash flow, signaling an industry-wide shift toward heavy physical-infra investment.

#### Background

Data-center power capacity is measured in gigawatts \(GW\) of connected electrical load, and high-density AI workloads require both significantly more power per rack and advanced liquid cooling to manage thermal output. China has historically been viewed as lagging behind North America in AI physical compute, but the rapid retrofit cycle of legacy retail机房 into AI-optimized facilities is closing that gap at scale.
