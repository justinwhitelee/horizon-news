#### Summary

Anthropic has completed its initial public offering on the Nasdaq in a first-of-its-kind listing for a leading AI lab, making it one of the world&\#x27;s most valuable publicly traded companies. The stock opened at $111.50 and closed at $97.17, up 17.07% from its IPO price of $83 but down 12.85% intraday. Reports cite a debut market capitalization near $2 trillion, though some commentary suggests the valuation has retreated from earlier $2T-plus expectations and now falls below OpenAI&\#x27;s private-market target valuations.

#### Background

Anthropic is a California-based artificial intelligence research company founded in 2015 that focuses on developing safe and beneficial AI systems, most notably the Claude large language model series. The company has historically been privately held and backed by investors including Google, Amazon, and NVIDIA. Its direct listing on the Nasdaq represents an unusual path to going public, bypassing the traditional initial public offering process that involves underwriters setting a fixed offer price.

#### Community Discussion

HackerNews commenters noted significant concerns among software engineers about AI displacing programming roles, with several calling it a historic turning point. Some pointed out the gap between pre-IPO hype valuations and the current trading level, suggesting implications for OpenAI&\#x27;s own valuation ambitions.
