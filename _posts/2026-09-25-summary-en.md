### [New cryptanalytic approach breaks RSA faster than known methods](https://arstechnica.com/security/2026/09/theres-a-new-way-to-break-rsa-thats-faster-than-anything-weve-seen-before/) ⭐️ 8.0/10



rss · Ars Technica · Sep 24, 11:15

#### Summary

Ars Technica reports a novel cryptanalytic approach that can break RSA encryption faster than any previously known method, moving beyond the traditional reliance on integer factorization. Until now, cryptographers assumed that factoring was the only viable path to compromising RSA, but this new technique challenges that assumption. The finding is technically significant because RSA underpins widespread encryption infrastructure, and an alternative faster attack could shift practical threat models for deployed systems. Details remain limited in the current report.

#### Background

RSA is a public-key cryptosystem whose security has historically depended on the computational difficulty of factoring large integers into their prime components. Decades of research have produced increasingly efficient factoring algorithms—most notably the general number field sieve—but no equally effective alternative method has been demonstrated until now.
