### [Mathematics has changed. Let&\#x27;s act like it](https://terrytao.wordpress.com/2026/11/01/mathematics-has-changed-lets-act-like-it/) ⭐️ 8.0/10



hackernews · smilelamp · Nov 1, 18:28 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50328251)

#### Summary

Terence Tao published an essay arguing that the mathematical community needs to adapt its norms around authorship and credit now that AI can produce complete proofs. The core claim is that someone who selects a worthwhile problem, obtains a proof using AI, thoroughly understands it, and develops further directions has made a substantial mathematical contribution—even without supplying new ingredients in the original proof. Tao has been articulating this position on digesting AI-generated results for several months. The broader shift reframes the mathematician&\#x27;s role from proof-writing craftsman to problem-selector and interpreter of AI-generated results.

#### Community Discussion

HN commenters drew parallels to industrial transformation, comparing the shift to the passage from craftsmanship to mass production. Some argued the community hasn&\#x27;t yet adapted to the full potential of the tool and &quot;dreams too small.&quot; There was debate about whether LLM agents will also handle higher-level tasks like choosing which terrain to explore, with one commenter noting that nearly all articles assume capabilities won&\#x27;t extend beyond theorem proving. A notable concern from practicing mathematicians was that AI-generated proofs tend to be ugly, long, and incomprehensible, though some responded that early proofs of major results are often like that and elegance comes later.
