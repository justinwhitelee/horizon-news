### [OpenAI reports ten advances in mathematics and theoretical computer science](https://openai.com/index/ten-advances-in-mathematics/) ⭐️ 8.0/10



hackernews · milkshakes · Aug 3, 16:27 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49157930)

#### Summary

OpenAI has published an index detailing ten advances in mathematics and theoretical computer science achieved with AI assistance, marking a notable expansion of AI&\#x27;s role beyond software engineering into formal proof generation and conjecture testing. The work demonstrates that large language models can both generate potential mathematical solutions and verify their validity, making previously intractable problems more computationally accessible. This development has drawn significant attention from the technical community, with Hacker News discussion reaching 390 score and 673 comments.

#### Background

The advances span areas such as high-dimensional sphere packing and multicolor Ramsey numbers, problems that have long resisted complete solution. AI-assisted mathematics represents a growing intersection where large language models are used not just for code generation but for formal reasoning tasks that require both creative conjecture and rigorous verification.

#### Community Discussion

Hacker News commenters noted that LLMs have made math proofs more computable by enabling computers to both generate and independently check solutions, though they cautioned this does not mean all of mathematics is now solved. Some discussed the broader implication that any computable problem will eventually fall to computers, while others drew parallels to how AI is displacing years of prior human research in specific domains. A few commenters pointed to specific problems like sphere packing and Ramsey numbers as surprisingly intuitive cases where AI assistance proved effective.

### [Qwen Releases 3.8-Max: 2.4T-Parameter Open-Source Model](https://qwen.ai/blog?id=qwen3.8) ⭐️ 8.0/10



telegram · zaihuapd · Aug 3, 02:31

#### Summary

The Qwen team has released Qwen 3.8-Max, a 2.4 trillion parameter model with 95 billion active parameters, marking the first time the team has open-sourced weights at its Max tier. Built on the Qwen 3.5 architecture, the model delivers significant improvements in coding, work, research, and long-horizon tasks. It demonstrated the ability to autonomously run for over 10 days to build and self-evolve a project, and won the WWW2025 multimodal dialogue intent recognition competition by defeating 458 of 526 teams within 24 hours. Model weights will be open-sourced next week, and the model is currently available via QwenCloud API.

#### Background

Qwen \(Tongyi Qianwen\) is a family of large language models developed by Alibaba&\#x27;s Tongyi Lab. The Max tier represents the largest and most capable models in the lineup, typically featuring sparse architectures with massive total parameter counts but fewer active parameters per token. Open-sourcing Max-tier weights is significant because such large models are typically kept proprietary due to the substantial compute and infrastructure required for training and inference.
