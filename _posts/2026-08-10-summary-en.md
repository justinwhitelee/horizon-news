### [vLLM v0.27.0 release introduces Kimi K3 support and PyTorch 2.13 upgrade](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/releases/tag/v0.27.0) ⭐️ 8.0/10



github · khluu · Aug 10, 21:18

#### Summary

vLLM v0.27.0 is a major release of the open-source LLM inference framework, featuring 561 commits from 242 contributors. Key additions include full-stack support for Kimi K3 models, an upgrade to PyTorch 2.13.0, and FlashAttention 4 FP8 KV cache support on SM100 GPUs. The release also introduces new model integrations such as Qwen3.5, K-EXAONE-2.0-750B-A37B, and VaultGemma, along with performance optimizations for DeepSeek-V4 and expanded Model Runner V2 capabilities for non-generative workloads.

#### Background

vLLM is a widely used open-source library for serving large language models, known for its high-throughput inference and efficient memory management. This release continues its focus on expanding model support and hardware compatibility while improving performance and scalability for production deployments.

### [Sony and TSMC Plan $6.4B Joint Venture for Image Sensors in Japan](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-10/sony-tsmc-to-invest-6-4-billion-in-joint-chip-plant-in-japan) ⭐️ 8.0/10



telegram · zaihuapd · Aug 10, 04:01

#### Summary

Sony and TSMC plan to invest approximately 1 trillion yen \(about $6.4 billion\) in a joint venture to build next-generation image sensor production facilities at Sony&\#x27;s semiconductor plant in Kumamoto, Japan. The合资企业 will be structured with Sony holding roughly 60% and TSMC 40%, with mass production targeted to begin as early as 2029. The sensors are designed for embodied AI applications, including robotics, automotive systems, and high-performance cameras. Both companies expect to finalize the investment agreement in the near term and establish the joint venture before the end of their fiscal year in March 2027, while also discussing potential government subsidies with Japan&\#x27;s Ministry of Economy, Trade and Industry.
