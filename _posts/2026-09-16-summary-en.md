### [US Military Confirms First On-Orbit Space Weapons Deployed](https://arstechnica.com/space/2026/09/for-the-first-time-the-us-military-confirms-it-has-deployed-weapons-in-orbit/) ⭐️ 9.0/10



rss · Ars Technica · Sep 15, 03:47

#### Summary

The United States military has publicly confirmed for the first time that it has deployed on-orbit space control weapons capable of defending the joint force, according to an Ars Technica report by Stephen Clark. This marks the inaugural acknowledgment of such a capability by the U.S. armed forces, representing a significant milestone in defense technology and space policy. The deployment signals the beginning of a new era in which space-based weapons systems are operationally active in orbit rather than remaining theoretical or development-stage concepts.

#### Background

The 1967 Outer Space Treaty prohibits placing weapons of mass destruction in orbit, but does not explicitly ban conventional weapons, creating a legal gray area that nations have exploited. Prior to this announcement, various countries including the US, Russia, and China had tested antisatellite \(ASAT\) missiles capable of destroying objects from the ground, but none had publicly confirmed deploying weapons that operate while already in orbit. The emergence of on-orbit space control weapons—systems designed to defend friendly satellites or deny adversaries use of space—marks a significant shift from ground-based counter-space capabilities to persistent orbital military presence.
