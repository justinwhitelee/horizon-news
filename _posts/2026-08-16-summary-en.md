#### Summary

Stripe has agreed to acquire AI firm OpenRouter for over $7 billion, according to Bloomberg. The acquisition represents a significant consolidation move in the AI infrastructure and developer tooling space, bringing together a widely used payments and developer infrastructure platform with an AI model routing and API aggregation service. The deal signals growing interest from established technology companies in owning the middleware layer that sits between developers and large language model providers. No additional details about the deal structure, regulatory approvals, or integration plans were provided in the available reporting.

#### Background

OpenRouter is an AI model routing and API aggregation platform that allows developers and companies to switch between different AI models through a single interface. Stripe is a widely used developer infrastructure company that provides payment processing and financial tools for internet businesses. The acquisition represents one of the largest deals in the AI infrastructure space, signaling continued consolidation as major platforms compete to own critical pieces of the AI development stack.
