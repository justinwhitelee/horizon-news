### [BDH-CQ: Recurrent Latent Reasoning for In-Context Learning](https://www.reddit.com/r/MachineLearning/comments/1vov5r5/bdhcq_incontext_learning_with_recurrent_latent/) ⭐️ 8.0/10



reddit · r/MachineLearning · /u/moschles · Aug 15, 06:18

#### Summary

Researchers introduced BDH-CQ, a reasoning system that performs in-context learning through recurrent latent computation without updating model parameters at inference. Demonstrations continuously update a recurrent memory, after which queries are solved iteratively in a high‑dimensional latent workspace with no verbalized intermediate states. A 150M‑parameter configuration reaches 29.5% pass@2 on ARC‑AGI‑1 at a computed cost of $0.00070 per task, breaking the previously reported cost–accuracy Pareto frontier. Neither task identifiers nor evaluation‑task demonstration pairs participate in training, making memory, adaptation, and inference part of a single computational fabric.

#### Background

ARC‑AGI‑1 is a benchmark designed to evaluate abstract reasoning ability, often viewed as a stepping stone toward artificial general intelligence. In‑context learning enables a model to adapt to a new task by processing demonstration examples without changing its training weights, offering a low‑cost alternative to fine‑tuning for specialized problems.
